# Speaking Module — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Tasks 9–12 are content tasks.

**Goal:**
- Speech-to-text in the sidecar, with tap-to-talk recording and temporary, scope-bound storage of speech.
- An estimated pronunciation read-out.
- Spoken practice in four places: lesson spoken responses, spoken exam parts (the AI as partner and examiner, graded against oral rubrics and anchors), Freestyle spoken conversation, and placement spoken items.

**Architecture:**
- **Sidecar:** faster-whisper, exposed as `/transcribe`.
- **App side:** `SpeechProvider.transcribe`, a `speech_clips` table plus `speech-tmp` files keyed by scope (deleted on scope end and after 6 h), and a `useRecorder` hook.
- **Pure logic:** grading reuses the writing rubric machinery (rubrics, `Mark`s, anchors) through pure functions in `lib/speaking/`.
- **Spoken Teil parts:** a server-side turn loop (one AI call per turn), with replies rendered by Piper into temporary clips.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, React 19, better-sqlite3, vitest 5; Python faster-whisper (sidecar).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-speaking-design.md`

**Precondition:** everything up to and including Writing is merged.

## Global Constraints

- **Transcription:** German (`language="de"`), `int8` on the CPU. The model comes from the admin setting `whisper_model` (`small` default, or `medium`). Audio is at most 5 minutes.
- **Storage:** speech lives only in `speech_clips` and `<data dir>/speech-tmp/`. It's deleted by `DELETE /api/speech/scope` when the scope ends, and by the worker after 6 h. It's never included in backups.
- **Scopes:** `lesson:<lessonId>`, `freestyle:spoken`, `teil:<attemptId>`, `placement`.
- **Pronunciation:** the score is the mean word probability × 100, and the unclear threshold is 0.6. The bands are "clear" ≥ 85 and "mostly clear" ≥ 70, else "sometimes unclear". It's always labelled as an estimate.
- **`spoken_response`:** a record-again loop before submitting, and a typed fallback without an estimate. Not in the Daily Queue, and not test-out eligible.
- **Spoken Teile:**
  - no prep phase in practice;
  - the examiner voice is `kerstin`, and the partner voice is chosen by gender and never the examiner's;
  - the loop ends at `turns` or on Finish;
  - grading uses the part's `rubric` and 2–3 anchors;
  - marks go into the error log with source `speaking`.
- **Placement:** spoken items only when the speech service is healthy at session start; otherwise they're left out of the served exam, and the thresholds are computed from what's served.
- **Existing rules:** `res.ok` and `role="alert"`; `delayedResponse`; student text in en and de; admin in English.

## Review Focus

1. **Microphone permission denied:** the student gets the explanation (HTTPS or localhost) and, in lessons, the typed fallback. It isn't a silent failure (Task 4).
2. **Tapping stop twice:** a double-tap on stop uploads once (Task 4).
3. **Scope cleanup when the tab closes:** clips of a lesson run abandoned by closing the tab are removed by the 6-hour cleanup (Task 2).
4. **An AI examiner that ends too early:** the partner or examiner reply never ends the conversation before `turns` unless the student presses Finish. An AI reply of `speaker: "examiner"` mid-way is played, but doesn't close (Task 3).
5. **Speech service down during placement:** a placement session started while the service is down never serves a spoken item, even if the service comes back mid-session (Task 8).

```python
from faster_whisper import WhisperModel

_models: dict[str, WhisperModel] = {}
ALLOWED_MODELS = {"small", "medium"}
MAX_SECONDS = 300


def whisper(name: str) -> WhisperModel:
    if name not in ALLOWED_MODELS:
        raise HTTPException(400, detail={"error": f"unknown model {name}"})
    if name not in _models:
        _models[name] = WhisperModel(name, device="cpu", compute_type="int8")
    return _models[name]


@app.post("/transcribe")
async def transcribe(request: Request, model: str = "small"):
    data = await request.body()
    if not data:
        raise HTTPException(400, detail={"error": "empty audio"})
    wav = ffmpeg(["-i", "pipe:0", "-ac", "1", "-ar", "16000", "-f", "wav", "pipe:1"], data)
    duration = (len(wav) - 44) / (16000 * 2)
    if duration > MAX_SECONDS:
        raise HTTPException(400, detail={"error": "audio longer than 5 minutes"})
    with tempfile.NamedTemporaryFile(suffix=".wav") as f:
        f.write(wav)
        f.flush()
        segments, info = whisper(model).transcribe(f.name, language="de", word_timestamps=True, vad_filter=True)
        words = [{"word": w.word.strip(), "start": w.start, "end": w.end, "probability": w.probability} for s in segments for w in (s.words or [])]
    return {"text": " ".join(w["word"] for w in words).strip(), "language": info.language, "duration": duration, "words": words}
```

  - README: the model setting, and that the `medium` model downloads on first use (~1.5 GB).
