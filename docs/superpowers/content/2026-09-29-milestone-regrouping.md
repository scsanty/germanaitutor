# Milestone regrouping — review summary

Every bundled track+level (seed version 4) is regrouped from the six skill buckets into 3–4 difficulty-ranked milestones. Rank 1 opens first; each later rank opens once every lesson of the rank before it is done. No ranks are shared (no parallel milestones). Every prerequisite sits in the same milestone or an earlier one.

Each lesson is listed as *title (skill)*. Lesson titles are unchanged, so B2 and C1 titles are still German.

## Generic

**2. Shopping and daily routine** — Talk about food, prices and your day, use the accusative and say no with nicht and kein.

**3. Questions and word order** — Build statements and questions with correct word order, use separable verbs, and read and hear short everyday texts.

- Separable-Prefix Verbs in the Present Tense (grammar)
- Word Order in Declarative Sentences and Questions (grammar)
- Listening to Public Announcements (listening)

**4. Introductions and invitations** — Say what you can and would like to do, introduce yourself aloud and write a short invitation.

- Modal Verbs: Können and Möchten (grammar)
- Spoken Personal Introduction (speaking)
- Writing Simple Messages and Invitations (writing)
